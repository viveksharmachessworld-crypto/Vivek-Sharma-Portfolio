# Owner image archive inventory

## Published images

The public site serves 31 optimized WebP images from `public/images/` (about 10.2 MB total). They are stored in this repository and served from the portfolio's own domain, so visitors do not need access to Google Drive. They comprise the profile portrait, all 14 certificate scans, 14 press/archive images and two chess/event photographs. The archive excludes the three personal photos the owner asked to keep private, including the solo award portrait, and excludes motorbike/car photos. Original uploads are kept out of version control and out of the deployed asset tree.
